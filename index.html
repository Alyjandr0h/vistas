      How it works

      • Save puts the photo in your Gallery and in Saved here. Saved one by mistake? Tap the heart again.
      • Back brings back a photo you missed.
      • Share sends it by text message or WhatsApp.
      • Screen saver plays the photos full screen. Tap anywhere to stop.
      • Play music shuffles songs from jw.org. Open it to pick a playlist or any song in it, and tap the heart on songs you love. Tap the photo there to save or share it.

      Tip: turn your phone sideways to see the photos bigger.
